Market brief

Your analyst reads the news on AI in BPM, hiring and client moves, and tells you how to prepare.

Slow down. You are safe right now.

  1. Breathe out slowly, longer than you breathe in. Do this 5 times.
  2. Name 5 things you see, 4 things you feel, 3 things you hear, 2 things you smell, 1 thing you taste.
  3. Go to your quiet room: picture a calm place you know well. Stay there for 1 minute.
  4. Remember one past success. Feel how you felt that day.
  5. Pick one small next action. Only that one.

These exercises help many people feel calmer, but they do not replace care. If anxiety stays strong or stops your day, talk to a doctor or counsellor. In India, Tele-MANAS is free and open all day: call 14416.
